# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and
knows something you don't.

This is a small project. It does not need a long document to say that abuse,
harassment and bigotry are not welcome here, and that people who insist on them
will be removed.

## What is expected

- Treat people with respect, including people you disagree with.
- Criticise code, decisions and ideas. Do not criticise the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to your change, and that this is not a
  judgement about you.
- Remember that most people here are writing in their second or third language,
  and that terseness is not rudeness.

## What is not

- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Slurs or demeaning remarks about anyone's race, gender, sexuality, disability,
  religion, nationality, appearance or age.
- Sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption — derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to make
  a point, or arguing in bad faith.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer, privately, at the address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are read by one person and kept
confidential. You will not be asked to justify being uncomfortable.

The maintainer will respond in a way they judge proportionate: a word in
private, a deleted comment, a temporary block, or a permanent one. Being a
frequent contributor earns no leeway here, and neither does being new.

## About broadcasters

One thing specific to this project. Station operators sometimes arrive in the
issue tracker because of something the app does with their stream — most often
to ask to be taken out of it. They are not users, they did not ask to be
involved, and they are entitled to a quick and friendly answer.

A removal request is honoured without argument and without asking for a reason.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the station policy in
`docs/EXECUTION.md`.
